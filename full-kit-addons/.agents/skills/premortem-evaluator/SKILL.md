# Premortem Evaluator (`/premortem-evaluator`)

Imagine it is 6 months from now and this initiative failed. Work backward to prevent the most likely failure modes today.

## Output Structure
1. **Top 5 Failure Modes**: Ranked by likelihood x impact (technical, financial, human/communication, timeline, scope creep).
2. **Early Warning Signals**: Concrete signs that each risk is starting to materialize.
3. **Low-Cost Mitigations**: Practical safeguards to put in place before starting.
4. **Go / Pause / Adjust Recommendation**: Candid verdict with clear reasoning.
