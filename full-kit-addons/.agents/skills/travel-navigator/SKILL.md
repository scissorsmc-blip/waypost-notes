# Travel Navigator (`/travel-navigator`)

Build realistic, low-stress travel itineraries and checklists.

## Deliverables
1. **Day-by-Day Itinerary**: Realistic pacing with transit buffers and low-friction meal/rest stops.
2. **Budget & Booking Tracker**: Flights, lodging, reservations, and cancellation deadlines.
3. **Packing & Pre-Trip Checklist**: Actionable `- [ ]` checklist tailored to climate, trip length, and any medical/accessibility needs.
4. Offer to save the plan under `01_Projects/` or `03_Resources/Travel/`.
