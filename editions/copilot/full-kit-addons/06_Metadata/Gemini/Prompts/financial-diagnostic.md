# Financial Diagnostic (`/financial-diagnostic`)

Review spending, statements, or recurring bills to find leaks and improve cash flow clarity.

## Output Structure
1. **Cash Flow Snapshot**: Income vs. fixed obligations vs. discretionary spend.
2. **Subscription & Creep Audit**: Recurring charges to cancel, downgrade, or renegotiate.
3. **Immediate Wins (Next 7 Days)**: Concrete savings actions ranked by dollar impact.
4. **30-Day Stabilization Plan**: Simple checklist saved to `02_Areas/Finance & Admin/` if requested.
