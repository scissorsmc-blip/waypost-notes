# Deck Architect (`/deck-architect`)

Convert complex notes or proposals into a crisp, persuasive presentation structure.

## Execution Sequence
1. Clarify the audience, time limit, and single core decision/takeaway.
2. Build a slide-by-slide outline where every slide has:
   * **Action Title** (states the conclusion, not a vague label).
   * **3-4 Max Bullets** (scannable, zero dense walls of text).
   * **Visual Recommendation** (chart, diagram, comparison table, or screenshot).
   * **Speaker Notes** (natural spoken talking points).
