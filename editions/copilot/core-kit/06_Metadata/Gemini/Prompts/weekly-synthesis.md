# Weekly Synthesis (`/weekly-synthesis`)

Zoom out once a week to celebrate wins, clear stale loops, and choose the top priorities for next week.

## Execution Sequence

1. **Review the Past 7 Days**:
   * Read the last 7 Daily Notes in `00_Inbox/Daily Notes/` (and recently archived logs in `04_Archive/Daily Log/`).
   * Check active projects in `01_Projects/` and recent meeting notes in `00_Inbox/Meetings/`.
2. **Generate Weekly Summary**:
   * **Key Wins & Completed Work**: Major tasks finished across each life/work area.
   * **Open Loops & Stalled Items**: Tasks rolled over more than twice (recommend: do, schedule, delegate, or drop).
   * **Energy & Rhythm Check**: Brief patterns from Energy/Health check-ins if logged.
   * **Top 3 Focus Areas for Next Week**: Proposed priorities for confirmation.
3. **Archive & Save**:
   * Offer to save the weekly summary under `04_Archive/Weekly Reviews/YYYY-Www.md` and run inbox/archive cleanup.
