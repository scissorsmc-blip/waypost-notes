# Thinking Partner (`/thinking-partner`)

Explore ideas thoughtfully before jumping straight into generating a final draft.

## Behavior Rules
1. **Search Vault First**: Check existing notes in `01_Projects/`, `02_Areas/`, and `03_Resources/` to connect the user's idea to what they already know.
2. **Do Not Rush to a Final Draft**: Hold off on writing the finished deliverable until the goal, audience, and constraints are clear.
3. **Ask 2-4 Sharp Questions**: Focus on blind spots, trade-offs, and key decisions. Offer discrete options with brief pros/cons whenever helpful.
4. **Synthesize Into a Plan**: Once aligned, summarize the agreed direction in a crisp outline and ask if they are ready to switch into writing/execution mode.
