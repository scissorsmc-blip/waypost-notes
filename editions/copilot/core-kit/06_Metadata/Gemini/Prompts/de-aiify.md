# De-AIify (`/de-aiify`)

Rewrite drafts so they sound like a real, thoughtful human wrote them.

## Editing Standards
1. **Strip AI Cliches & Buzzwords**: Remove words and filler phrases like "delve", "tapestry", "beacon", "testament", "crucial", "leverage", "landscape", "in summary", "it is important to note", and "unlock the power of".
2. **Active Voice & Plain English**: Prefer short, clear sentences, concrete nouns, and direct verbs over bloated corporate phrasing.
3. **Match User Tone**: Follow the tone preferences recorded in `AGENTS.md` (including punctuation preferences such as avoiding em-dashes if specified).
4. **Preserve Meaning**: Keep all facts, numbers, links, and intent intact. Do not invent claims.
5. **Output Cleanly**: Provide the revised text ready to copy-paste, followed by a brief 2-3 bullet note on the main improvements if helpful.
